# **2\. ALUMINUM TYPES, SIZES, AND PRICING**

## **2.1 Aluminum Sizes**

The aluminum profile dimensions are measured in **inches**.

The currently identified aluminum sizes are:

* 1" × 1"  
* 1" × 2"  
* 1" × 3"  
* 1¾" × 3"  
* 1¾" × 4"

One whole regular aluminum profile has a standard stock length of:

> **6 meters**

## **2.2 Aluminum Pricing Rule**

With the client's confirmation, R.R.D.'s standardized aluminum price will be computed as:

> **R.R.D. Aluminum Price \= Current Supplier Price × 2**

Supplier prices may change; therefore, the supplier price should be **updatable by the authorized admin**.

## **2.3 Current Aluminum Reference Prices**

| Aluminum Size | Supplier Price / 6m | R.R.D. Price ×2 | R.R.D. Price per Meter |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 1" × 1" | ₱360 | ₱720 | ₱120.00/m |
| 1" × 2" | ₱520 | ₱1,040 | ₱173.33/m |
| 1" × 3" | ₱800 | ₱1,600 | ₱266.67/m |
| 1¾" × 4" | ₱1,200 | ₱2,400 | ₱400.00/m |
| 1¾" × 3" | ₱900 | ₱1,800 | ₱300.00/m |

## **2.4 Aluminum Computation Process**

### **Step 1 — Get the Current Supplier Price**

Use the latest supplier price of the selected aluminum profile.

### **Step 2 — Compute the R.R.D. Price**

> **Supplier Price × 2 \= R.R.D. Price**

### **Step 3 — Compute the Price per Meter**

Since one whole regular aluminum profile is **6 meters**:

> **R.R.D. Price ÷ 6 \= R.R.D. Price per Meter**

### **Step 4 — Compute the Required Aluminum Cost**

> **Required Length in Meters × R.R.D. Price per Meter \= Aluminum Cost**

### **Sample Computation**

Selected aluminum:

> **1" × 1"**

Supplier price:

> ₱360 per 6-meter length

R.R.D. price:

> ₱360 × 2 \= **₱720**

Price per meter:

> ₱720 ÷ 6 \= **₱120/m**

If the project requires 2 meters:

> 2m × ₱120 \= **₱240**

Therefore:

> **Aluminum Cost \= ₱240**

## **2.5 Aluminum System Rules**

> **R.R.D. Aluminum Price \= Current Supplier Price × 2**

> **R.R.D. Price per Meter \= R.R.D. Aluminum Price ÷ 6**

> **Aluminum Cost \= Required Length × R.R.D. Price per Meter**

> **White / Analok \= Standard Product Price**

> **Special / Powder-Coated Color \= Standard Product Price × 2**

## **3.3 Premium Glass Pricing Rule**

If the customer selects a premium glass **type OR color**, the standard/original product price will be multiplied by 2\.

> **Premium Glass Price \= Standard Product Price × 2**

The multiplier should only be applied **once**, even if both the glass type and glass color are premium.

### 

### **System Rules**

> **IF Glass Type \= Tempered OR Reflective → Premium \= YES**

> **IF Glass Color \= Silver OR Blue → Premium \= YES**

> **IF Premium \= YES → Standard Product Price × 2**

> **IF Glass Type and Glass Color are both Standard → No ×2 multiplier**

### **Example 1 — Premium Type \+ Premium Color**

Customer selects:

* Glass Type: **Tempered**  
* Glass Color: **Silver**  
* Standard Price: **₱5,000**

Calculation:

> ₱5,000 × 2 \= **₱10,000**

Even though both selections are premium, the ×2 multiplier is applied **only once**.

### **Example 2 — Standard Type \+ Standard Color**

Customer selects:

* Glass Type: **Regular**  
* Glass Color: **Clear**  
* Standard Price: **₱5,000**

No premium selection is present.

> **Final Price \= ₱5,000**

# **4\. GLASS MATERIAL COSTING**

## **4.1 Supplier Glass Cost**

R.R.D. purchases glass from the supplier as a **whole sheet**.

To determine the usable price per square foot:

> **Whole Sheet Area \= Width in Feet × Height in Feet**

> **Supplier Price per Sq.Ft. \= Whole Sheet Supplier Price ÷ Whole Sheet Area**

### **Sample — Clear Glass**

Whole glass sheet:

> **4 ft × 6 ft**

Supplier price:

> **₱864**

### **Step 1 — Calculate Total Square Feet**

> 4 × 6 \= **24 sq.ft.**

### **Step 2 — Calculate Supplier Price per Square Foot**

> ₱864 ÷ 24 \= **₱36/sq.ft.**

Therefore:

> **Clear Glass Supplier Cost \= ₱36/sq.ft.**

## **4.2 Project Glass Cost**

Once the required glass area is known:

> **Glass Material Cost \= Required Glass Area × Glass Price per Sq.Ft.**

Example:

If a project requires 10 sq.ft. of the sample glass:

> 10 × ₱36 \= **₱360**

Therefore:

> **Glass Material Cost \= ₱360**

# **5\. GLASS THICKNESS RULES**

## **5.1 Available Thicknesses**

The applicable glass thicknesses are:

* **6mm**  
* **8mm**  
* **12mm**

### **3mm Glass**

3mm glass should **not** be included as a regular selectable thickness for the products covered by GlassFit.

According to R.R.D., 3mm glass is too thin and is more commonly associated with applications such as picture frames.

---

## **5.2 Glass Thickness Pricing**

### **6mm**

6mm is considered the standard/default thickness.

> **Additional Thickness Charge \= ₱0**

### **8mm**

If the selected glass type and color are standard:

> **Additional Charge \= ₱400**

If the selected glass type OR color is premium:

> **Additional Charge \= ₱600**

### **12mm**

If the selected glass type and color are standard:

> **Additional Charge \= ₱1,000**

If the selected glass type OR color is premium:

> **Additional Charge \= ₱1,200**

## **5.3 Glass Thickness Pricing Matrix**

| Thickness | Standard Glass | Premium Glass |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 6mm | \+₱0 | \+₱0 |
| 8mm | \+₱400 | \+₱600 |
| 12mm | \+₱1,000 | \+₱1,200 |

**Standard Glass:** Neither the type nor color triggers premium pricing.

**Premium Glass:** The selected type and/or color triggers the ×2 premium pricing rule.

# **6\. OVERALL GLASS PRICING LOGIC**

### **Step 1 — Determine the Standard Product Price**

Calculate the normal/base price of the selected product.

### **Step 2 — Check the Glass Type**

Premium types:

* Tempered  
* Reflective

### **Step 3 — Check the Glass Color**

Premium colors:

* Silver  
* Blue

### **Step 4 — Apply Premium Multiplier**

If either the glass type or color is premium:

> **Standard Product Price × 2**

Apply the multiplier **only once**.

### 

### **Step 5 — Apply Thickness Charge**

| Thickness | Standard | Premium |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 6mm | \+₱0 | \+₱0 |
| 8mm | \+₱400 | \+₱600 |
| 12mm | \+₱1,000 | \+₱1,200 |

### **Complete Example**

Customer configuration:

* Standard Product Price: ₱5,000  
* Glass Type: Tempered  
* Glass Color: Silver  
* Thickness: 8mm

**Step 1 — Premium Pricing**

Tempered and Silver trigger premium pricing.

> ₱5,000 × 2 \= **₱10,000**

**Step 2 — 8mm Premium Thickness Charge**

> ₱10,000 \+ ₱600 \= **₱10,600**

Therefore:

> **Computed Price \= ₱10,600**
