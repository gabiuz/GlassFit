## 1. Outcome and Scope

In initial fiducial implementations (IMP-MS45 and IMP-MS46), paper detection was tightly coupled to an explicit paper standard string passed from the client, and edge detection frequently broke when room lighting created shallow wall shadows or low contrast against white or off-white paint. Consequently, uploads often failed to resolve high-confidence candidates, falling back to manual corner pinning.

`IMP-MS48` enhances the FastAPI computer vision microservice (`fastapi-service/paper_calibration.py` and `main.py`) to make fiducial detection completely autonomous, highly resilient to real-world room lighting, and accurate to millimeter precision:

1. **Autonomous Standard Resolution:**
   - Evaluates candidate quadrilaterals against all standard Philippine paper formats without requiring pre-flight customer selection:
     - **Short Bond Paper (US Letter):** 215.9 mm x 279.4 mm (Aspect Ratio 1.2941)
     - **A4 Standard Paper (ISO 216):** 210.0 mm x 297.0 mm (Aspect Ratio 1.4143)
     - **Long Bond Paper (Philippine Folio):** 215.9 mm x 330.2 mm (Aspect Ratio 1.5294)
   - Evaluates both upright (portrait) and sideways (landscape) mountings automatically.

2. **Illumination-Invariant Multi-Stage Segmentation:**
   - **Stage 1 (HSV & Luminance Isolation):** Extracts paper regions by combining HSV white segmentation (low saturation $S < 45$, high value $V > 180$) with bilateral edge-preserving smoothing (`d=7`, `sigmaColor=50`, `sigmaSpace=50`).
   - **Stage 2 (Multi-Path Edge & Morphology):** Fuses adaptive Gaussian thresholding with Otsu-guided Canny edge filtering. Applies morphological closing (`cv2.morphologyEx`) with a 5x5 structuring element to reconnect contours broken by tape strips or shadows.
   - **Stage 3 (Adaptive Epsilon Quadrilateral Fitting):** Uses an iterative `cv2.approxPolyDP` sweep across epsilon factors (0.015, 0.020, 0.025, 0.035) to resolve true 4-vertex quadrilaterals even when paper edges are slightly worn or taped.
   - **Stage 4 (Geometric Validation Cascade):** Validates convexity (`cv2.isContourConvex`), area bounds (0.05% to 15% of photo area), interior corner angles (60 to 120 degrees), and aspect ratio matching (within 18% perspective tolerance).
   - **Stage 5 (Sub-Pixel Corner Refinement):** Applies `cv2.cornerSubPix` on the 4 detected corners using a 5x5 search window, eliminating pixel quantization jitter and achieving physical precision.
   - **Stage 6 (Homography & Coplanar Metric Scale):** Calculates the Direct Linear Transform (DLT) 3x3 planar homography matrix ($H$) and derives the physical scale factor (`scale_cm_per_px`) for the wall plane.

3. **Autonomous Confidence Scoring:**
   - Confidence >= 0.65 qualifies as an automatic detection success, setting `status="detected"` and bypassing manual mapping modals on the client.
   - Confidence < 0.65 gracefully returns `status="manual_pin_required"` with best candidate hint corners without throwing HTTP 500 errors.

### 1.1 Non-Negotiable Invariants

1. **Deterministic Computer Vision:** The system relies exclusively on deterministic OpenCV (`cv2`) and NumPy (`numpy`) algorithms without introducing external neural network weights or third-party ML packages.
2. **Strict Time Budget:** Paper detection must execute in under 45ms on a 1024x1024 normalized image, preserving the 2,500ms total microservice response budget (`QAD-TC4`).
3. **Graceful Fallback:** Low-contrast images or photos without paper must never raise unhandled exceptions. The endpoint returns `status="manual_pin_required"` or `status="not_requested"` with `confidence=0.0`.
4. **Orientation Invariance:** The engine must accurately detect paper mounted in either portrait or landscape orientation without false rejections.
5. **No Em-Dashes:** In compliance with `BAN-PUNCT-01`, all documentation, docstrings, and code comments must use hyphens, colons, or parentheses.
6. **No Box Diagrams in Code Blocks:** All pipeline descriptions and candidate cascades must use standard Markdown tables and ordered prose (`BAN-DIAG-03`).

## 2. Mathematical Formulation & Geometric Constraints

### 2.1 Canonical Philippine Paper Metric Definitions

Standard paper dimensions in millimeters are defined as $(W_m, H_m)$ in canonical upright orientation:

| Standard Identifier | Metric Width ($W_m$) | Metric Height ($H_m$) | Canonical Aspect Ratio ($H_m / W_m$) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `short` (US Letter) | 215.9 mm | 279.4 mm | 1.2941 |
| `a4` (ISO 216) | 210.0 mm | 297.0 mm | 1.4143 |
| `long` (Folio) | 215.9 mm | 330.2 mm | 1.5294 |

When paper is mounted sideways (landscape), the canonical aspect ratio is $W_m / H_m$, the reciprocal of the upright ratio.

Canonical metric destination coordinates in centimeters (origin at top-left corner) are:
- Top-Left: $P_0 = (0, 0)$
- Top-Right: $P_1 = (W_{\text{cm}}, 0)$
- Bottom-Right: $P_2 = (W_{\text{cm}}, H_{\text{cm}})$
- Bottom-Left: $P_3 = (0, H_{\text{cm}})$

Where $W_{\text{cm}} = W_m / 10$ and $H_{\text{cm}} = H_m / 10$.

### 2.2 Planar Homography Transformation

Given four ordered pixel coordinates $p_i = (u_i, v_i)$ for $i \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, the projective mapping between image pixel coordinates and the metric wall plane is represented by a 3x3 planar homography matrix $H$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} x_{\text{metric}} \\ y_{\text{metric}} \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \sim H \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} h_{11} & h_{12} & h_{13} \\ h_{21} & h_{22} & h_{23} \\ h_{31} & h_{32} & h_{33} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

$H$ is calculated via `cv2.getPerspectiveTransform(src_pts, dst_pts)` using the Direct Linear Transform (DLT) algorithm.

### 2.3 Coplanar Scale Factor Derivation

In addition to $H$, a scalar metric scale factor `scale_cm_per_px` is derived for linear dimension grounding:

1. Compute edge lengths in pixel space:
   - $\text{topWidth}_{\text{px}} = \|p_1 - p_0\|$
   - $\text{bottomWidth}_{\text{px}} = \|p_2 - p_3\|$
   - $\text{leftHeight}_{\text{px}} = \|p_3 - p_0\|$
   - $\text{rightHeight}_{\text{px}} = \|p_2 - p_1\|$

2. Geometric mean edge lengths:
   - $\text{avgWidth}_{\text{px}} = \sqrt{\text{topWidth}_{\text{px}} \cdot \text{bottomWidth}_{\text{px}}}$
   - $\text{avgHeight}_{\text{px}} = \sqrt{\text{leftHeight}_{\text{px}} \cdot \text{rightHeight}_{\text{px}}}$

3. Directional scale factors:
   - $\text{scale}_W = W_{\text{cm}} / \text{avgWidth}_{\text{px}}$
   - $\text{scale}_H = H_{\text{cm}} / \text{avgHeight}_{\text{px}}$

4. Global coplanar scale:
   - $\text{scale}_{\text{cm\_per\_px}} = (\text{scale}_W + \text{scale}_H) / 2$

## 3. Computer Vision Detection Algorithm

The detection pipeline is implemented in `fastapi-service/paper_calibration.py` through the following structured stages:

### 3.1 Pipeline Stages Summary

| Stage Number | Stage Name | Operational Method | Primary Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Luminance & Color Pre-Processing | Grayscale conversion, bilateral filter (`d=7`, `sigmaColor=50`, `sigmaSpace=50`), and variance check | Suppresses high-frequency wall texture while preserving sharp sheet borders |
| Stage 2 | Multi-Threshold Segmentation | Dual-path: Adaptive Gaussian thresholding (`blockSize=21`, `C=5`) and Otsu Canny edge detection | Generates binary edge masks under varied interior illumination |
| Stage 3 | Morphological Closure | Morphological close with 5x5 rectangular structuring element | Bridges gaps in paper perimeter caused by mounting tape or shallow shadows |
| Stage 4 | Contour Extraction & Polygon Fit | `cv2.findContours` followed by multi-pass `cv2.approxPolyDP` with epsilon factors (0.015, 0.020, 0.025, 0.035) | Extracts candidates and resolves imperfect boundaries into 4-vertex quadrilaterals |
| Stage 5 | Candidate Filter Cascade | Convexity check, area bounds (0.05% to 15%), interior angles (60 to 120 deg), aspect ratio matching | Rejects non-paper wall fixtures, picture frames, and irregular objects |
| Stage 6 | Sub-Pixel Corner Refinement | `cv2.cornerSubPix` with 5x5 window and termination criteria (max 30 iterations, epsilon 0.01) | Snaps vertices to exact physical sheet corners |
| Stage 7 | Canonical Ordering | Vertex sorting by coordinate sum and difference: [Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Right, Bottom-Left] | Guarantees consistent point indexing for homography calculation |
| Stage 8 | Homography & Scale Math | `cv2.getPerspectiveTransform` and average edge scale calculation | Produces 3x3 homography matrix and scalar `scale_cm_per_px` |
| Stage 9 | Confidence Assessment | Evaluates aspect ratio agreement, angle perpendicularity, and edge gradient strength | Returns `status="detected"` if confidence >= 0.65; otherwise returns `status="manual_pin_required"` |

### 3.2 Autonomous Paper Standard Matching Logic

When `paper_standard` is omitted or set to `"auto"`, candidate contours are evaluated against all valid Philippine standards:

```python
def _match_best_paper_standard(
    measured_aspect: float,
    preferred_standard: str | None = None,
) -> tuple[str, float]:
    """
    Finds the closest Philippine paper standard matching the measured aspect ratio.
    Returns (matched_standard, aspect_error).
    """
    standards = ["short", "a4", "long"]
    if preferred_standard and preferred_standard in standards:
        target_aspect = _get_target_aspect(preferred_standard)
        error = abs(measured_aspect - target_aspect) / target_aspect
        return preferred_standard, error

    best_standard = "short"
    min_error = float("inf")

    for std in standards:
        target_aspect = _get_target_aspect(std)
        error = abs(measured_aspect - target_aspect) / target_aspect
        if error < min_error:
            min_error = error
            best_standard = std

    return best_standard, min_error
```

### 3.3 Sub-Pixel Corner Optimization

To eliminate discretization errors from pixel grids:

```python
def _refine_subpixel_corners(
    gray: np.ndarray,
    ordered_corners: np.ndarray,
) -> np.ndarray:
    """
    Refines corner coordinates using gradient zero-crossing within a 5x5 window.
    """
    criteria = (
        cv2.TERM_CRITERIA_EPS + cv2.TERM_CRITERIA_MAX_ITER,
        30,
        0.01,
    )
    refined = cv2.cornerSubPix(
        gray,
        ordered_corners.copy(),
        winSize=(5, 5),
        zeroZone=(-1, -1),
        criteria=criteria,
    )
    return refined
```

## 4. FastAPI Pipeline Integration

In `fastapi-service/main.py`, the `/analyze-image` route handler coordinates paper detection with the existing analysis pipeline:

1. **Ingest Form Parameters:**
   - `calibration_mode: str = Form("auto_detect")`
   - `paper_standard: str | None = Form(None)`

2. **Conditional Execution:**
   - If `calibration_mode in {"auto_detect", "paper_fiducial"}`:
     - Invoke `detect_paper_fiducial(image_bgr, paper_standard)`.
     - Record `paper_calibration` payload containing status, corners, scale, confidence, and homography matrix.
   - If `calibration_mode == "standard"`:
     - Skip paper detection completely to preserve raw upload performance.

3. **Workspace Dimension Rescaling:**
   - If the workspace image was normalized (e.g. resized from 4000x3000 to 1024x768), scale corner coordinates and scale factor proportionally:
     $$\text{corners}_{\text{scaled}} = (x \cdot \text{scale}_X, y \cdot \text{scale}_Y)$$
     $$\text{scale}_{\text{workspace}} = \text{scale}_{\text{original}} / ((\text{scale}_X + \text{scale}_Y) / 2)$$

4. **Scale Signal Override:**
   - When paper detection yields `status="detected"` with `confidence >= 0.65`:
     - Set `scale_result.best_scale_cm_per_px = paper_calibration["scale_cm_per_px"]`.
     - Set `scale_result.confidence = paper_calibration["confidence"]`.
     - Set `scale_result.method = f"paper_fiducial_{matched_standard}"`.

## 8. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Glare or harsh sunlight washes out paper edges | Medium | Multi-mask approach (Adaptive Gaussian + Otsu Canny + Morphological Closing) tests multiple binary segmentations to find closed contours. |
| User photographs paper at a steep angle (> 45 degrees) | Medium | Interior angle tolerance allows 60 to 120 degrees, and aspect ratio tolerance accommodates up to 18% projective distortion. If distortion exceeds limits, graceful fallback to manual pinning is triggered. |
| Rectangular light switch or electrical plate mistaken for paper | Low | Area bounds (minimum 0.05% of photo area) and strict aspect ratio matching against standard paper dimensions reject small wall fixtures. |
