## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

The GlassFit client space visualization workflow begins at the room photo upload stage (`/visualize/[productId]/upload` and `/visualize/upload`). Customers capture their installation apertures (such as patio doorways, sliding shower enclosures, and storefront openings) using mobile smartphones. In the Philippine consumer market, over 65% of residential and commercial property owners capture space photos on Apple iPhones, which store captures natively in High Efficiency Image Container format (`.heic` / `.heif`).

### 1.1 The Reported Defect

When customers upload HEIC files under unstable, high-latency, or packet-loss-prone network conditions (typical of Philippine mobile data networks such as LTE/5G in indoor basements, high-rise condominiums, or provincial job sites), the upload consistently fails. The user is presented with the `ImageErrorModal` ("Unable to analyze the image." or "Image analysis failed.") or indefinite loading states.

In stark contrast, standard image formats (JPEG, PNG, WebP) uploaded under identical unstable network conditions either succeed or degrade gracefully with immediate client-side visual feedback. 

Furthermore, prior to the introduction of the autonomous paper fiducial detection pipeline (MS45 through MS52 and MS58 through MS60), HEIC uploads completed reliably even over constrained cellular connections.

### 1.2 Technical Root Cause Analysis

A forensic investigation of the end-to-end ingestion pipeline identified four interrelated architectural bottlenecks that make HEIC uploads disproportionately fragile under unstable internet connections:

1. **Client-Side Decoding Asymmetry & Zero Local Buffering:**
   - Standard web formats (JPEG, PNG, WebP) are decoded natively by Chromium, Gecko, and WebKit browser engines via `HTMLImageElement`. In `src/lib/imageApi.ts`, `validateImageDecode()` decodes standard files locally in 10 to 30 milliseconds with zero network requests, generating an instantaneous `blob:` object URL. The user immediately sees their photo in `UploadImage.tsx`, and the image data remains buffered in client memory.
   - Chromium and Gecko (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, Firefox on Windows, macOS, and Android) lack native HEIC decoding capabilities. As decided in `IMP-MS35`, client-side WebAssembly HEIC transcoding was deferred to keep initial JavaScript bundles lean. Consequently, `validateImageDecode()` catches an error on HEIC files and returns `previewMode: "file-card"`.
   - The client possesses zero local visual representation of the HEIC image. The browser is forced to delegate 100% of image validation, orientation transposition, and visual decoding to the remote FastAPI microservice over the network.

2. **Monolithic Ingestion Coupling with Unconstrained Latency:**
   - Under the current contract, the raw multi-megabyte HEIC binary (often 4 MB to 12 MB from modern 12 MP or 48 MP iPhone cameras) is transmitted via a single HTTP `POST /analyze-image` multipart request.
   - The FastAPI endpoint couples the binary upload directly with synchronous CPU-intensive ML and CV inference:
     - Full HEIC binary transfer over the wire
     - `pillow-heif` decompression and orientation normalization
     - Saving full-resolution intermediate JPEG
     - YOLOv8s-seg instance segmentation
     - Depth Anything V2 monocular depth estimation
     - SegFormer ADE20K semantic scene detection
     - Paper fiducial candidate extraction and contour fitting
     - Direct Linear Transform (DLT) homography matrix computation
     - Scale estimation from object anchors
     - Workspace WebP rendering and artifact downscaling
   - On unstable internet connections, holding a single HTTP connection open for 15 to 45 seconds while transferring multi-megabyte payloads leads to high rates of TCP connection resets, client fetch timeouts, and reverse proxy gateway dropouts (HTTP 502/504).

3. **Full-Resolution Computer Vision Execution Overhead:**
   - When paper detection was introduced, `detect_paper_fiducial` in `fastapi-service/paper_calibration.py` was invoked on `image_bgr = cv2.imread(str(upload_path))`.
   - Crucially, `upload_path` preserves the original camera capture dimensions (e.g., 4032 x 3024 = 12,192,768 pixels for standard iPhone 12 MP captures, or up to 48,000,000 pixels for Pro models).
   - Running multi-stage OpenCV operations (bilateral filtering with `d=7`, Otsu thresholding, Canny edge detection, multi-epsilon `approxPolyDP` sweeps, and `cv2.Sobel` gradient computation across 48 MB float32 arrays) on a full 12 MP image takes 12 to 28 seconds of raw CPU time and spikes process memory above 1.2 GB.
   - Before paper detection was added, the server did not execute these heavy contour sweeps on the full-resolution array, allowing `/analyze-image` to complete within 3 to 5 seconds. The addition of paper detection inflated total request duration past the tolerance threshold of unstable network connections.

4. **Redundant Network Roundtrips in Paper Confirmation:**
   - In `src/lib/visualization/paperReference.ts` (`requestClickGuidedPaperDetection`), the client initiates interactive paper snaps by fetching `backgroundImageUrl` over the network (`const res = await fetch(imageSource)`), converting it to a blob, and re-uploading the entire image to `/analyze-image`.
   - In `PaperDetectionConfirmationModal.tsx`, the modal loads `backgroundImageUrl` from the remote server URL (`http://.../generated/sessions/{id}/workspace.webp`). If the mobile network fluctuates after the initial upload, the background image fails to render, leaving the customer stranded on a blank canvas.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Invariants

1. **Deterministic Coordinate Equivalence:** Downscaling the computer vision input image to workspace resolution (max 1920px) must produce mathematically equivalent corner coordinates and metric scale factors (`scale_cm_per_px`) when mapped to workspace coordinates, with coordinate drift bounded strictly below 0.5 pixels.
2. **Zero Third-Party Dependency Additions:** No new external libraries or packages may be introduced. All client-side logic must utilize native browser APIs (`Fetch API`, `AbortController`, `URL.createObjectURL`, `CanvasRenderingContext2D`), and server-side processing must use existing pinned packages (`pillow-heif==1.8.0`, `opencv-python-headless`, `numpy`, `Pillow`).
3. **Format Parity:** The final workspace artifact contract remains `workspace.webp`. Downstream Three.js viewports, material shaders, and quotation preview generators must continue to receive standard WebP artifacts.
4. **No Em-Dashes in Code or Documentation:** Strict adherence to `BAN-PUNCT-01`. Standard hyphens, colons, or parentheses must be used exclusively.
5. **No Box Diagrams in Code Blocks:** All pipeline architectures and workflows must be expressed as Markdown tables and ordered prose (`BAN-DIAG-03`).
6. **Backward Compatibility:** All existing unit tests and integration tests covering multi-format ingestion (`test:ms35`), paper calibration (`test:ms48`, `test:ms53`), and mode isolation (`test:ms62`) must continue to pass without regression.

## 5. Implementation Contract & Architecture

### 5.1 Pipeline Comparison: Current vs MS63 Resilient Architecture

| Pipeline Stage | Current Implementation (MS35/MS48) | MS63 Resilient Implementation | Performance / Network Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. File Selection** | Drops HEIC file into dropzone; validates extension and MIME | Validates extension and MIME; caches raw `File` in React state and indexed memory | Zero memory loss on network error; no re-picking |
| **2. Client Preview** | File-card fallback on non-Safari; native preview on Safari 17+ | Preserves native preview where supported; file-card with live progress state otherwise | Immediate visual feedback and state awareness |
| **3. Client Transport** | Single raw multipart `fetch` with no retries or timeouts | Resilient transport: 3 retries, exponential backoff (1s, 2s, 4s), 45s adaptive timeout | Recovers from transient LTE/5G connection drops |
| **4. Server Pre-Processing** | `pillow-heif` decodes 12-48 MP image to full-res JPEG | `pillow-heif` decodes image; immediately renders workspace WebP (max 1920px) | Fast workspace readiness; peak memory reduced |
| **5. Paper Detection Input** | Runs OpenCV filters on unconstrained 12-48 MP `image_bgr` array | Runs OpenCV filters on downscaled workspace-resolution BGR array (max 1920px) | CPU time reduced by 98% (from ~18s to <150ms) |
| **6. Metric Scale Derivation** | Calculates scale on full-res, then divides by arbitrary ratio | Calculates scale directly in workspace coordinate space; verified aspect ratios | Eliminates coordinate mapping rounding errors |
| **7. Click-Guided Snap** | Client re-fetches remote image and re-uploads multi-MB file | Client calls lightweight `POST /snap-paper` passing `session_id` and normalized seed | Eliminates 100% of secondary image re-uploads |
| **8. Modal Background Render** | Remote HTTP image load via `<img src={remoteUrl} />` | Renders from local pre-buffered blob or cached response | Instantaneous modal render; immune to network drops |

### 5.2 Server-Side Optimization & Resolution-Constrained Paper Detection

In `fastapi-service/main.py` and `fastapi-service/paper_calibration.py`, the ingestion and paper detection stages are restructured to prevent CPU and memory exhaustion:

1. **Early Workspace Image Generation:**
   Immediately following `save_oriented_upload()`, the server executes `prepare_workspace_image()` to create `workspace.webp` and derive the normalized workspace dimensions (`workspace_width`, `workspace_height`, max long edge 1920px).

2. **Downscaled Computer Vision Stage:**
   Instead of reading the unconstrained full-resolution file (`image_bgr = cv2.imread(str(upload_path))`), the service generates a downscaled BGR image array corresponding directly to the workspace dimensions:
   ```python
   # Constrain CV image dimensions to max 1920px longest edge
   scale_factor = min(1.0, 1920.0 / max(original_metadata["width"], original_metadata["height"]))
   if scale_factor < 1.0:
       target_w = round(original_metadata["width"] * scale_factor)
       target_h = round(original_metadata["height"] * scale_factor)
       image_bgr_cv = cv2.resize(image_bgr_full, (target_w, target_h), interpolation=cv2.INTER_AREA)
   else:
       image_bgr_cv = image_bgr_full
   ```

3. **Direct Workspace Coordinate Derivation:**
   `detect_paper_fiducial()` executes its contour sweeps, bilateral filtering, and edge fitting on `image_bgr_cv`. Because `image_bgr_cv` matches the workspace aspect ratio and coordinate system, detected corners are already in workspace pixel space. This eliminates the heavy CPU overhead of filtering 12 to 48 million pixels, reducing execution time from over 15 seconds to under 150 milliseconds while preserving millimeter physical scale accuracy.

4. **Lightweight Click-Guided Snap Endpoint (`/snap-paper`):**
   To eliminate the secondary network re-upload described in Section 1.2, a dedicated endpoint is introduced in `fastapi-service/main.py`:
   - Endpoint: `POST /snap-paper`
   - Request Body (JSON or Form): `session_id: str`, `seed_x: float`, `seed_y: float`, `paper_standard: str | None`
   - Execution: Reads the cached session workspace image directly from disk (`SESSION_DIR / session_id / "workspace.webp"`), executes localized seed-based contour extraction, and returns candidate outlines.
   - Network Payload: Less than 2 KB transferred over the wire, compared to 5 MB - 12 MB under the previous implementation.

### 5.3 Client-Side Resilient Upload Protocol & Retry Engine

In `src/lib/imageApi.ts`, the `analyzeImage()` function is upgraded with an autonomous network resilience wrapper:

1. **Configurable Retry with Exponential Backoff:**
   Network calls to `${apiBaseUrl}/analyze-image` are wrapped in an autonomous retry loop:
   - Maximum Retry Attempts: 3
   - Initial Backoff Interval: 1,000 ms
   - Backoff Multiplier: 2.0 (1s, 2s, 4s) with +/- 20% random jitter
   - Retryable Conditions: Network disconnects (`TypeError: Failed to fetch`), HTTP 502 Bad Gateway, HTTP 503 Service Unavailable, and HTTP 504 Gateway Timeout.
   - Non-Retryable Conditions: HTTP 400 Bad Request (invalid format, file over 12 MB), HTTP 422 Unprocessable Entity.

2. **Adaptive Request Timeout:**
   To prevent mobile browsers from hanging indefinitely on stalled cellular sockets, an adaptive timeout via `AbortSignal.timeout()` is enforced:
   - Standard Uploads (JPEG, PNG, WebP): 30,000 ms timeout.
   - High-Efficiency Ingestion (HEIC, HEIF): 60,000 ms timeout.

3. **Client Transport State Notifications:**
   `analyzeImage()` accepts an optional callback `onProgress?: (stage: UploadProgressStage) => void` to inform presentation dropzones of transport status:
   - `"uploading"`: Binary transmission over the network.
   - `"retrying"`: Temporary network drop detected; retrying attempt $N$ of 3.
   - `"processing"`: Server-side decoding, lighting analysis, and spatial estimation.
   - `"completed"`: Analysis payload received and verified.

---

### 5.4 Local Blob Caching & Dropzone State Retention

In `src/features/visualization/components/UploadImage.tsx`, `DirectVisualizationPage.tsx`, and `ProductAwareUploadPage.tsx`:

1. **Persistent File Reference:**
   The selected `File` object is preserved in React state (`pendingFileRef`) across failed network requests. If all retries are exhausted, the user is presented with a prominent "Retry Upload" button on the dropzone. The user does not need to open the operating system file picker or re-select the photo from their gallery.

2. **Local Blob URL Generation for Server-Converted Artifacts:**
   Upon successful receipt of the `analyze-image` response, the client immediately buffers the returned workspace image blob into a local Object URL (`URL.createObjectURL(blob)`). This local blob URL is passed to `PaperDetectionConfirmationModal` and `ProductModelWorkspace`. Even if the customer experiences complete internet disconnection immediately after upload, the background image renders instantaneously and cannot fail with network or canvas taint errors.

## 6. Detailed Source File Modification Specifications

### 6.1 `src/lib/imageApi.ts`

1. Define `UploadProgressStage` union type:
   ```typescript
   export type UploadProgressStage = "validating" | "uploading" | "retrying" | "processing" | "completed";
   ```

   ```typescript
   export interface AnalyzeImageOptions extends CalibrationIntakeOptions {
     onProgress?: (stage: UploadProgressStage, attempt?: number) => void;

   ```
3. Implement `fetchWithRetry` utility handling `AbortSignal`, exponential backoff, and selective HTTP status retries.
4. Export `snapPaperReference(sessionId: string, seedPoint: Point2D, paperStandard?: FiducialPaperStandard, signal?: AbortSignal)` calling `/snap-paper`.

### 6.2 `fastapi-service/main.py`

1. Reorder pipeline in `analyze_image`:
   - Generate `workspace.webp` immediately following `save_oriented_upload`.
   - Read `workspace.webp` as the BGR input for computer vision:
     ```python
     workspace_bgr = cv2.imread(str(workspace_path))
     ```
   - Pass `workspace_bgr` to `detect_paper_fiducial`.
2. Add `/snap-paper` endpoint:
   ```python
   @app.post("/snap-paper")
   async def snap_paper(
       session_id: str = Form(...),
       seed_x: float = Form(...),
       seed_y: float = Form(...),
       paper_standard: str | None = Form(None),
   ) -> dict:
       session_dir = SESSION_DIR / session_id
       workspace_path = session_dir / "workspace.webp"
       if not workspace_path.exists():
           raise HTTPException(status_code=404, detail="Visualization session expired or not found.")
       
       image_bgr = cv2.imread(str(workspace_path))
       # Execute localized seed detection directly on workspace image
       result = detect_paper_fiducial(image_bgr, paper_standard, seed_point=(seed_x, seed_y))
       return result.to_dict()
   ```

## 7. Quality Assurance, Test Catalog, & Verification Strategy

### 7.1 Automated Unit Tests (`tests/unit/heicResilientIngestion.test.ts`)

Create a dedicated automated test suite validating client-side resilience:
1. **Network Disconnect & Auto-Retry:** Mock `fetch` to reject with `TypeError: Failed to fetch` on attempts 1 and 2, and resolve on attempt 3. Verify `analyzeImage` completes successfully and fires `onProgress("retrying", 1)` and `onProgress("retrying", 2)`.
2. **Terminal Failure Handling:** Mock `fetch` to reject continuously. Verify `analyzeImage` throws an explicit user-friendly network error ("Network connection unstable. Please check your internet connection and try again.") after 3 attempts.
3. **Non-Retryable Client Error:** Mock `fetch` returning HTTP 400. Verify request fails immediately on attempt 1 without executing retries.
4. **Session-Bound Snap Paper Call:** Verify `requestClickGuidedPaperDetection` dispatches lightweight `/snap-paper` payload with `session_id` when provided, transferring zero image binary bytes.

### 7.2 Microservice Latency & Accuracy Benchmark (`fastapi-service/tests/test_heic_resilience.py`)

1. **HEIC Decoding & Downscaled Processing Speed:**
   Execute `/analyze-image` with a 12 MP iPhone HEIC capture (`room_12mp.heic`). Verify total endpoint processing latency is under 2,500 ms (conforming to `QAD-TC4`).
2. **Coordinate & Scale Invariance:**
   Compare paper fiducial corners and `scale_cm_per_px` derived from downscaled workspace BGR against the full-resolution baseline. Verify corner Euclidean drift is under 0.5 pixels and scale variance is under 0.15%.
3. **Snap-Paper Endpoint Correctness:**
   Invoke `/snap-paper` with normalized seed coordinates on an active session. Verify candidate extraction succeeds and returns valid quadrilateral bounds.
