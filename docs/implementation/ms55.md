## 1. Outcome and Scope

Prevent the third supplied photo from producing a trusted wall dimension merely because the held paper was detected correctly. A valid transform measures the paper's plane. It cannot be applied to a wall at unknown depth and orientation without additional geometric information.

Under the single-photo workflow, a plain sheet held away from the wall does not supply enough evidence for a dependable general correction. The supported recovery is to retake with the sheet flat on the installation plane, enter both tape-measured opening dimensions, or continue with a clearly approximate preview. Manual corner mapping alone cannot resolve plane mismatch.

The plane restriction follows the planar correspondence model in the [OpenCV homography tutorial](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d9/dab/tutorial_homography.html). This milestone does not introduce a depth sensor, multi-view reconstruction, calibrated camera workflow, or continuous AR tracking.

## 2. Verified Starting Point

Existing calibration metadata combines successful paper detection, user corner approval, and a verified status. The current scalar measurement branch bypasses depth correction when paper calibration is present. No independent per-opening paper-plane eligibility contract is present in `CalibrationMetadata`.

Existing upload guidelines advise the same wall, but instructions alone do not prevent a held or adjacent-wall sheet from being accepted. The correction must reach the domain calculation and quotation eligibility, not only help text.

## 3. Non-Negotiable Invariants

1. Distinguish four facts: a sheet was found, its physical size is known, its corners were reviewed, and its plane applies to this opening.
2. User approval of a paper outline is not proof of coplanarity or measurement accuracy.
3. Relative monocular depth and YOLO person overlap may provide warnings; they do not establish absolute plane displacement or a reliable transfer scale. No guessed depth-ratio correction may promote a mismatched reference to metric status.
4. A session may contain multiple installation planes. Eligibility attaches to the opening/product plane, not all overlays in the image.
5. Preserve approximate visualization and explicit manual measurement as supported choices. Do not force successful calibration to enter the workspace.

## 4. Eligibility and Recovery Contract

### 4.1 Proposed State Separation

Store these as explicit, validated fields alongside the versioned MS54 geometry contract. Names are proposed additions, not claims about current API fields.

| Field | Values or meaning |
|---|---|
| `placement_assessment` | `unknown`, `user_confirmed_same_plane`, or `mismatch_reported` |
| `placement_risk_reasons` | Evidence such as handheld placement reported, adjacent surface, unknown offset, occlusion, or warped paper |
| `target_plane_id` | Identity binding one installation plane to the reference and eligible overlays |
| `metric_eligibility` | `needs_size`, `needs_placement_confirmation`, `geometry_invalid`, `plane_mismatch`, or `eligible_planar_estimate` |
| `measurement_source` | Planar photo estimate, manual tape, or heuristic preview, as mapped in MS56 |

Domain eligibility requires a confirmed format and orientation correspondence, valid MS54 geometry, explicit same-plane placement confirmation for the target, and no unresolved placement mismatch. Do not infer same-plane status from `user_verified`, detector confidence, proximity in the photo, or legacy `verified_calibrated`.

### 4.2 Placement Review

Use the existing paper review surface to show the automatic outline and ask whether the sheet was flat against the installation surface when photographed. Reuse current controls and layout. Concise copy: "Was the paper flat against the same surface as the opening? Holding it in front can change the measurements."

| Answer or condition | Result | Available next steps |
|---|---|---|
| Flat against the same installation plane | Record user declaration and bind target plane; still apply geometry gates | Continue with planar estimate |
| Held in front, another wall, or known offset | Set plane mismatch; do not publish metric paper-derived opening dimensions | Retake, manual width and height, approximate preview |
| Unsure | Remain unconfirmed | Show placement guidance, then retake or other recovery |
| Outline correct but paper curled or partly hidden | Geometry remains ineligible if boundary/flatness evidence is insufficient | Correct visible corners only when defensible, otherwise retake |
| Another product placed on a different surface | Require its own plane assignment and reference or manual dimensions | Preserve scene without inheriting incompatible calibration |

The system need not automatically identify every handheld sheet. A user declaration is the minimum required placement evidence. Existing person masks can trigger advisory review but cannot alone prove mismatch or invalidate a valid wall-mounted sheet near a person.

### 4.3 Capture Guidance

1. Place a known sheet flat on the intended installation plane, near the opening, with all four corners visible. Do not cover the boundary with fingers or fold the sheet.
2. Frame the entire opening and sheet with enough paper pixels for stable corner localization. Prefer a less oblique view when practical.
3. Define the measured perimeter: front installation surface versus recessed jamb or back wall. Same visual wall region does not guarantee the same physical plane.
4. If the installation plane cannot hold paper, collect both physical width and height manually. Do not infer missing metric dimensions from an arbitrary single known distance under unrestricted perspective.

Reuse current `GuideLine.tsx` and existing paper guidance assets. Do not copy the supplied private room photographs into `public/`.

## 5. Step-by-Step Implementation Plan

1. Add explicit eligibility evaluation to domain utilities and API/session contracts shared with MS53-MS54.
2. Add placement declaration and recovery actions to the existing confirmation workflow. Invalid geometry and plane mismatch must be distinguishable from missing paper.
3. Gate model measurement, dimension labels, quotation source, and session hydration on domain eligibility rather than visual badges.
4. Bind each fitted opening to an installation plane; invalidate that binding on explicit plane change or incompatible scene replacement.
5. Record the distinction between user-declared placement and independently validated physical accuracy in MS56 provenance.
6. Register QAD-TC70 and update capture/measurement acceptance wording during implementation only.

## 6. Verification and Acceptance Criteria

| Test ID | Scenario | Expected outcome | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| QAD-TC70.1 | Sample 3 with placement declared handheld | Metric opening estimate blocked even with perfect paper corners | P0 |
| QAD-TC70.2 | User maps all four handheld corners manually | Plane mismatch persists; no verified measurement promotion | P0 |
| QAD-TC70.3 | Flat sheet, known format, valid geometry, same-plane declaration | Eligible planar estimate for assigned target only | P0 |
| QAD-TC70.4 | Person overlaps or stands near wall paper | Advisory evidence does not alone determine plane status | P1 |
| QAD-TC70.5 | Different-wall secondary product | Existing reference cannot automatically establish its metric dimensions | P0 |
| QAD-TC70.6 | User chooses manual tape dimensions | Both dimensions accepted through existing guardrails with manual provenance | P0 |
| QAD-TC70.7 | Unsure placement or skip | Approximate preview remains usable without calibrated labels | P0 |

Planned tests cover the pure eligibility evaluator and upload, workspace, and quotation transitions. Physical mismatch fixtures belong to MS57. No implementation or runtime validation is part of this documentation change.

## 7. Risks and Mitigations

A customer may confirm the wrong plane. Keep the declaration auditable, explain the placement plainly, and label outputs as photo estimates. Manufacturing verification remains separate. Do not advertise that a single photo can reliably recover an unknown handheld-to-wall distance.
