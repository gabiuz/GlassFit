## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

In customized architectural glass and aluminum fabrication, pricing transparency and operational accuracy depend on matching software estimation with workshop realities.

### 1.1 The Glass Raw Material Pricing Model Limitation
Under previous milestones (`IMP-MS21` and `MS-5`), glass inventory in `public.raw_materials` assumed all glass is purchased and billed as whole sheets (e.g. 4 ft x 6 ft = 24 sq.ft.) and derived into square-meter (`sqm`) surface area costs. 

However, in Philippine workshop operations (especially for Jalousie Windows and louver assemblies), glass blades are frequently procured, stocked, and billed as discrete, pre-cut units (e.g., 4" x 24", 4" x 30", 4" x 36", 6" x 24" jalousie slats) on a **per-piece (`pc`)** basis. Forcing jalousie blades into sheet-area formulas introduces unnecessary scrap calculations and unit conversions. The administrative material catalog (`MaterialModal.tsx`, CSV batch importer, and Supabase data layer) must allow glass materials to be defined with a `pc` billing unit, and the parametric pricing engine must evaluate component quantities directly by blade count.

### 1.2 Automated Quotation Surcharges vs Raw Product Pricing
In earlier theoretical pricing engines (`pricingEngine.ts`), quotations automatically appended synthetic multipliers:
- A fixed labor floor of PHP 750.00 or 25% of direct materials.
- An automated contractor gross margin markup of 25%.

Per operational feedback from R.R.D. Aluminum and Glass Works, this automated markup structure creates discrepancies between customer expectations and actual workshop billing. Customer quotations should strictly reflect the **raw product price** (the combined cost of aluminum framing extrusions, glass infill/blades, hardware components, accessories, and finish multipliers). The software should not inject speculative labor or overhead fees into customer self-service quotations.

### 1.3 Administrative Booking Labor Entry Workflow
Fabrication labor, site delivery, scaffolding, old fixture demolition, and installation complexity cannot be reliably guessed by a mathematical formula before site inspection. Therefore:
- The customer self-service quotation and generated PDF consultation document output the deterministic raw product price.
- When the customer submits a booking consultation request, the booking record inherits the raw product quotation as its baseline.
- During booking review in the administrative portal (`src/features/admin/bookings/BookingsContent.tsx`), the administrator enters the verified **Labor & Installation Fee** (as well as any custom site discounts or surcharges) via the booking price editor.
- The effective final booking total dynamically updates: `Effective Booking Total = Raw Product Quotation + Admin Labor & Service Charges`.

## 3. Mathematical Pricing Architecture & Domain Invariants

### 3.1 Glass Raw Material Ingestion Models

Glass materials in GlassFit are categorized into two structural procurement models:

1. **Sheet Glass (Area-Based Derivation):**
   - Billing Unit: `sqm` (or derived from stock sheet feet `width_ft * height_ft`).
   - Use Case: Sliding windows, awning windows, fixed picture frames, shower enclosures, and swing doors.
   - Cost Equation:
     ```
     Glass_Area_sqm = Width_m * Height_m * Quantity
     Glazing_Cost = Glass_Area_sqm * Unit_Price_per_sqm
     ```

2. **Jalousie Glass Blades (Per-Piece Pricing):**
   - Billing Unit: `pc` (Per Piece).
   - Use Case: Jalousie window louvers, pre-cut slat ventilation panels, and modular glass louvers.
   - Cost Equation:
     ```
     Blade_Count = Base_Quantity (or derived: ceil(Aperture_Height_mm / Louver_Pitch_mm) * Panel_Count)
     Jalousie_Glass_Cost = Blade_Count * Unit_Price_per_pc
     ```

3. **Finish & Thickness Rules on Per-Piece Glass:**
   - Standard Jalousie Glass finishes: Clear, Bronze (`x1.0` multiplier).
   - Premium Jalousie Glass finishes: Frosted, Tinted Blue, Silver Reflective (`x2.0` premium multiplier applied to the base per-piece rate).
   - Standard Jalousie thickness: 6mm (+PHP 0.00).

### 3.2 Raw Product Quotation Rule (Zero Automated Markup Invariant)

The customer quotation represents strictly the **Direct Raw Product Price** (`P_raw_product`):

```
P_raw_product = C_aluminum + C_glass + C_hardware + C_consumables
```

Where:
- `C_aluminum`: Sum of all required extrusion lengths multiplied by their R.R.D. linear meter rate, adjusted for special powder-coating finish multiplier (`x2.0`) if selected.
- `C_glass`: Sum of sheet glass area cost (`Area * Rate_sqm`) or jalousie blade cost (`Blades * Rate_pc`), adjusted for single-application premium glass multiplier (`x2.0`) and thickness surcharge if selected.
- `C_hardware`: Sum of all locking latches, POM rollers, jalousie mechanisms/clips, friction stays, and handles (`Quantity * Unit_Price`).
- `C_consumables`: Fasteners, weatherstripping, sealant tubes, and setting blocks.

**The Quotation Invariant:**
```
Quotation_Final_Price = P_raw_product
Fabrication_Labor_Fee (Quotation Phase) = 0.00 (Omitted from automated calculation)
Contractor_Margin_Markup (Quotation Phase) = 0.00 (Omitted from automated calculation)
```

Customer quotations and consultation PDF handoffs state:
> "This quotation reflects the raw product and materials fabrication cost. Site labor, delivery, and installation fees will be assessed and confirmed upon site consultation."

### 3.3 Administrative Booking Labor & Final Price Lifecycle

When a booking request is processed by the administrator in `/admin/bookings`:

1. **Initial State (Pending Booking):**
   ```
   Booking_Calculated_Price = Quotation_Raw_Product_Price
   Booking_Effective_Price = Booking_Calculated_Price
   Admin_Labor_Charge = null
   ```

2. **Admin Price Adjustment Action:**
   The administrator opens the booking price editor and enters:
   - **Labor & Installation Fee** (`laborAmount`: e.g. PHP 1,500.00), OR
   - **Negotiated Item/Total Final Price** (`negotiatedFinalPrice`).

3. **Reconciled Effective Final Amount:**
   ```
   Booking_Effective_Price = Quotation_Raw_Product_Price + Admin_Labor_Charge (or Negotiated_Amount)
   ```

4. **Quotation Document Regeneration:**
   When the administrator or customer views the updated quotation PDF, the document displays:
   - Itemized Raw Product Fabrication Subtotal: `₱X,XXX.XX`
   - Professional Installation & Site Labor (Admin Confirmed): `₱Y,YYY.XX`
   - Effective Agreed Grand Total: `₱Z,ZZZ.XX`

### 4.2 Parametric Pricing Engine Refactoring (`src/lib/pricing/pricingEngine.ts`)

The parametric pricing engine must be updated to:
1. Recognize `comp.rawMaterial.billing_unit === "pc"` for glass/glazing components and compute `computedQty = comp.baseQuantity`.
2. Compute `finalQuotation = directMaterialsSubtotal` (omitting automatic labor floors and contractor margin additions from the raw customer quotation).

```typescript
// src/lib/pricing/pricingEngine.ts

export function calculateParametricBOM(
  components: ComponentPricingInput[],
  options: CalculateBOMOptions
): CalculatedBOMResult {
  // ... dimensional and scrap calculations ...

  // Direct Materials Subtotal (Aluminum + Glass + Hardware + Consumables)
  const directMaterialsSubtotal = round2(
    effectiveFramingCost + effectiveGlazingCost + hardwareSubtotal + consumablesSubtotal
  );

  // Per IMP-MS24: Customer quotation reflects raw product price without automated labor/margin additions
  const fabricationLaborCost = 0.00;
  const totalDirectCost = directMaterialsSubtotal;
  const contractorMargin = 0.00;
  const finalQuotation = directMaterialsSubtotal;

  const frozenDetails: FrozenPricingDetails = {
    width_m: round4(widthM),
    height_m: round4(heightM),
    panel_count: panelCount,
    has_sill: hasSill,
    finish_type: finishType,
    glass_type: glassDesc,
    items_breakdown: allBreakdown,
    raw_material_subtotal: round2(rawFramingSubtotal + rawGlazingSubtotal),
    waste_allowance_subtotal: round2(scrapFramingSubtotal + scrapGlazingSubtotal),
    direct_material_subtotal: directMaterialsSubtotal,
    labor_cost: 0.00,
    contractor_margin: 0.00,
    margin_rate: 0.00,
    total_estimate: finalQuotation,
  };

  return {
    // ... result fields mapping finalQuotation to directMaterialsSubtotal ...
    finalQuotation,
    fabricationLaborCost: 0,
    contractorMargin: 0,
    frozenDetails,
  };
}
```

## 5. UI & Administrative Workflows

### 5.1 Admin Raw Material Ingestion Modal (`MaterialModal.tsx`)

Enhance the Assisted Setup Mode in `MaterialModal.tsx`:
1. When **Category** is set to `Glass`:
   - Present a toggle or segmented button: **"Standard Whole Sheet (Area)"** vs **"Jalousie / Louver Blade (Per Piece)"**.
   - If **Standard Whole Sheet** is selected: Display Sheet Width (ft), Sheet Height (ft), and Sheet Stock Price (₱).
   - If **Jalousie Blade (Per Piece)** is selected: Set `billing_unit = "pc"`, hide sheet dimensions, and display a direct **"Price per Blade (₱/pc)"** input with optional blade dimension helper text (e.g. `4" × 24"`).

### 5.2 Admin Booking Request Labor Editor (`BookingsContent.tsx`)

Enhance the pricing card in `BookingsContent.tsx`:
1. **Raw Product Price Section:**
   - Displays the unmutated calculated raw product price (`₱X,XXX.XX`).
2. **Labor & Installation Charge Section:**
   - Displays current admin-inputted labor fee (or `Not specified - Pending admin review`).
   - "Add / Edit Labor Fee" button opens an inline input field allowing the administrator to specify the labor cost (e.g. `₱1,500.00`) and a labor description (e.g. `Second-floor installation & seal`).
3. **Effective Grand Total Summary:**
   - Displays the computed total: `Raw Product Price + Labor Charge`.
   - Action buttons: "Save Labor & Total", "Reset to Raw Price", "Preview Updated PDF".

### 5.3 Quotation Summary & PDF Display (`QuotationPdfGenerator.tsx`)

Update the customer quotation PDF template:
1. Title and header clearly state **"Glass & Aluminum Product Fabrication Quotation"**.
2. Itemized tables show fixture dimensions, profile specifications, glass type, and unit raw product price.
3. Total row shows **"Total Raw Product Price"**.
4. Labor row:
   - In customer quotation phase: Displays `"Installation & Site Labor: To be assessed upon site consultation"`.
   - In admin-confirmed booking phase: Displays `"Installation & Site Labor (Admin Confirmed): ₱Y,YYY.XX"`.
5. Grand Total reflects the effective agreed amount.
